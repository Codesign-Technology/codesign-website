About Codesign Technology

Simple. Useful. Engineered.

The Codesign approach

Codesign builds products from first principles. We focus on clarity, manufacturability, and the details that make a product feel intentional. Every decision is grounded in engineering discipline and real‑world constraints.

We remove complexity, refine the essentials, and design systems that are practical, reliable, and easy to understand. The goal is always the same: make things that work, make sense, and stand up to use.

Founder and background

Codesign is led by a founder‑engineer and businessman with hands‑on experience in international markets and across hardware, firmware, compliance, and enclosure design. The work is technical, detail‑driven, and grounded in the realities of manufacturing and product integration.

From early prototypes to production‑ready systems, the focus is on building products that are simple, robust, and engineered with intention.

Global vision

Codesign is engineered in Australia and built for the world. TowWave is the first platform in a long‑term, disciplined product roadmap designed for global standards and real‑world use.

As the company grows, the focus remains the same: clarity, usefulness, and engineering that respects the details.
